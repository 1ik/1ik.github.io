01

Foundation

Stage 1 · 100–160 hours Bridge into model systems
Prerequisite Day 0 gate + sustainable weekly schedule

Translate existing engineering judgment into Python, PyTorch, ML fundamentals, and observable model execution.

Production PythonPyTorchTransformersGPU memoryTesting
Project 01Model execution laboratory

Small open-weight model, token and tensor visibility, decoding comparison, TTFT, generation rate, memory, tests, raw data, and reproducible setup.

Readiness gate

Rebuild the core loop, explain tensor transformations, diagnose seeded defects, reproduce benchmarks, and complete a two-hour extension without AI implementation.

02

Parallel depth

Primary · 60–70% LLM inference engineering
Prerequisite Stage 1 model execution fluency
  • vLLM deeply; SGLang comparatively
  • TTFT, ITL, throughput, and tail latency
  • Batching, scheduling, KV cache, quantization
  • Profiling, overload, reliability, and cost

180–300 focused hours

Secondary · 30–40% RL & post-training foundations
Prerequisite Python + probability foundation
  • MDPs, returns, value, and policy
  • Tabular Q-learning across seeds
  • Policy gradients and PPO concepts
  • Reward design, evaluation, and GRPO

80–140 hours initially, overlapping later stages

Stage 2 deliverable · 8–12 weeks maintained Production inference benchmark service

Build an OpenAI-compatible service with representative workloads, streaming, authentication, quotas, cancellation, overload behavior, telemetry, failure recovery, and honest cost/performance analysis.

Proof

  • Raw benchmark data + environment manifest
  • p50/p95/p99, throughput, utilization, memory
  • Three releases and one postmortem
  • 3–5 substantive external contributions

Gate C

Show one reproducible improvement and one failure. A reviewer can operate the project from documentation alone.

03

Convergence

Stage 3 · 240–400 hours total Rollouts → verification → learning
Prerequisite Credible Stage 2 evidence; RL remains secondary until then

Join inference and RL in one measurable, recoverable system: versioned data, multiple rollouts, sandboxed verification, durable records, maintained post-training tooling, and held-out evaluation.

CapstoneInference + RL convergence system

Trace one sample end to end. Control duplicates, interruptions, leakage, reward hacking, uncertainty, regressions, cost, and GPU utilization.

Convergence gate

Explain how rollouts become training signals, recover the pipeline, compare baseline and changed model honestly, and justify distribution only with a measured bottleneck.

05

Destination

The honest destination

Trusted with ambiguous work.
Dependable when it gets difficult.

Not “someone who knows AI tools.” A remote engineer who turns uncertainty into reviewable work, ships observable systems, communicates risk early, and helps others move.

Experienced software engineer→AI infrastructure engineer→Distributed ML systems engineer→Post-training infrastructure specialist
Six months of sustained evidence matters more than one polished demo.

Decision points

Readiness is observable.

Apply in widening circles. Do not wait for mastery, and do not advance to hide weak foundations.

A

Independent learner

Can attempt, diagnose, explain, test, and sustain the work.

B

AI-backend transition applicant

Project 1 is reproducible; scoped work is possible without AI implementation.

C

Specialization applicant

Maintained inference evidence withstands technical questioning.

D

Dependable remote engineer

Delivery, updates, disagreement, recovery, and availability are proven.

E

Advanced contributor

Leads scoped systems through discovery, rollout, maintenance, and hard news.
